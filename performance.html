🔬 Key findings

1. 65% of the {{ total }} rows are exact duplicates; a random 70/30 split puts 81% of test rows in training too.

3. Removing Occupation raised honest accuracy: SVM reaches 75.2% (vs 71.7%). 22 identical profiles have conflicting labels, capping any model at 82.6%.
4. Blood pressure, sleep duration and age are the strongest predictors (SHAP).
